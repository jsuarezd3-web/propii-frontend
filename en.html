FAQ

Questions from businesses

How much does Propii cost for a business?

The Basic plan is free for the business. The Premium plan costs $3,999 MXN per property per month and includes the Google review engine and the team performance dashboard.

Does my business have to withhold income tax (ISR) or issue invoices for tips?

Tips are the worker's income and they are subject to ISR. With Propii, each tip goes straight to the worker's bank account and never passes through the business's register, so the business is no longer the middleman: it does not withhold ISR on tips, add them to payroll, or issue CFDI invoices for them.

Can tips be split among my team?

Yes. The business sets percentages by worker category and Propii splits each tip automatically at the moment of payment.

Do I need to buy card terminals or hardware?

No. Each worker uses their QR code, printed or on their phone.

How do guests pay the tip?

They scan the worker's QR code with their phone camera and pay by credit or debit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, with no app to download. The tipping page is available in English and Spanish.
